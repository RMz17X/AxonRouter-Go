# 1023 — Playground inventory deduped per (provider, model): the picker loads again at farm scale

## Symptom
- The Playground showed "No active connections. Enable a provider
  connection first" while staging held tens of thousands of active
  connections. GET /api/playground/models answered 200 with ~314k
  entries (~59 MB, ~3.5 s server-side): one entry per model PER
  CONNECTION, so a 31k-key provider farm duplicated its small model
  catalog tens of thousands of times and the browser never got a
  loadable list — the picker fell through to its empty state.

## Root cause
- The inventory deliberately attributed each model to every connection
  publishing it (fragment 1008 design, "grouped by provider +
  connection"), which was right at 19 connections and fatal at farm
  scale. The playground chat dispatch never pins a connection — the
  serving path picks the account — so the duplicates carried no
  capability, only payload.

## Fix
- playgroundInventory dedupes entries by (provider, model), keeping
  the first eligible connection as the attribution, and caps
  contributing connections per provider at 100
  (playgroundProviderConnectionCap): real fleets (dozens of accounts
  per provider, where per-account model pins matter) are covered in
  full; farm providers publish their catalog once. Payload drops from
  ~314k entries / ~59 MB to the few hundred distinct models.
